01CRM & Data

Salesforce CRM Transformation

Connecting lead acquisition, CRM data & marketing performance

OutcomeSalesforce became the central lead platform, with marketing and CRM data analysed together.

  • Salesforce
  • LeadSquared
  • Meta Ads
  • Google Ads
  • Meta Pixel
  • CAPI
  • APIs

Problem

Marketing data and CRM outcomes needed better integration to measure lead quality, track campaign performance, and ensure reliable lead capture.

Impact

  • Established Salesforce as a central platform for lead management.
  • Improved visibility into campaign performance and lead follow-up.
  • Enabled marketing and CRM data to be analysed together for better optimization decisions.
  • Strengthened lead capture and conversion tracking reliability.

My role: MarTech requirements, CRM configuration planning, cross-functional technical coordination, integration validation.

What I drove

  • CRM migration — coordinated the move from LeadSquared to Salesforce, covering historical and incoming lead data.
  • Marketing attribution — introduced custom Salesforce fields to track Meta and Google campaign performance and CRM calling accuracy.
  • System integrations — coordinated website and Meta Instant Form integrations with Salesforce, including API-based lead identifiers.
  • Conversion tracking — supported Meta Pixel and CAPI tracking to improve conversion signal availability.
  • Data reliability — validated lead flows and coordinated troubleshooting when integrations or tracking failed.
